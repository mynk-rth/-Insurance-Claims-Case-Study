# Data Exploratory Analysis and Hypothesis Testing for Insurance Claims Data

A short Jupyter-based case study analyzing insurance claims data through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and hypothesis testing.

## Project Overview

A short Jupyter-based case study analyzing insurance claims, customer segments, claim amounts, fraudulent claims, and relationships between different customer and claim attributes.

Goals:

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
- Analyze insurance claims and customer segments
- Explore claim amounts using visualizations
- Identify patterns in fraudulent and driver-related claims
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing
- Draw conclusions and derive business insights
